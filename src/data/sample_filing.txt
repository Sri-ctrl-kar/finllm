Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Our total net revenue for fiscal year 2025 was $394.2 billion, an increase of 6% compared to
the prior fiscal year. This growth was primarily driven by strong performance in our Services
segment, which grew 14% year-over-year to $96.2 billion, partially offset by a slight decline
in Products revenue in certain international markets due to unfavorable foreign exchange rates.

Gross margin for the year was 46.2%, up from 44.1% in the prior fiscal year, reflecting a
favorable shift in product mix toward higher-margin Services offerings and cost efficiencies
in our supply chain.

Operating expenses increased 8% year-over-year to $57.5 billion, primarily due to increased
headcount-related expenses and higher research and development investment in artificial
intelligence and machine learning initiatives.

Net income for fiscal year 2025 was $101.8 billion, compared to $97.0 billion in the prior
fiscal year, an increase of approximately 5%.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of the end of fiscal year 2025, we had $162.1 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and
marketable securities. We believe our existing balances of cash and cash equivalents,
along with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to satisfy our working
capital needs, capital asset purchases, dividends, and other liquidity requirements
associated with our operations over at least the next 12 months.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to a variety of risks, including but not limited to global and
regional economic conditions, which could materially adversely affect demand for our
products and services. Fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates have affected,
and may continue to affect, our reported revenue and operating margins.

We face substantial competition in all markets in which we operate, and this competition
could result in pricing pressure, reduced profitability, and loss of market share.

The Company's future performance depends in part on its ability to continue to develop,
manufacture, and market innovative products and services in a highly competitive
environment. Our investments in artificial intelligence carry execution risk, and there
is no assurance that these investments will generate returns in line with our
expectations, or that our AI-related products will be received favorably by customers
or achieve the level of adoption we anticipate.

We rely on a limited number of suppliers for certain components, and any disruption in
our supply chain, including due to geopolitical tensions or trade restrictions, could
materially and adversely affect our ability to meet customer demand.

Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

Total assets as of the end of fiscal year 2025 were $412.6 billion, compared to $391.4
billion in the prior fiscal year. Total liabilities were $267.3 billion, compared to
$258.5 billion in the prior fiscal year. Stockholders' equity was $145.3 billion.

Diluted earnings per share for fiscal year 2025 was $6.42, compared to $5.98 in the
prior fiscal year, an increase of approximately 7%, reflecting both higher net income
and the impact of ongoing share repurchases, which reduced diluted shares outstanding
by approximately 2% year-over-year.

The Company repurchased $89.4 billion of its common stock during fiscal year 2025 under
its share repurchase program, and paid cash dividends of $15.2 billion.
